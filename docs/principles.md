# What retromorphism is, and is not

## Is not

**Not skeuomorphism.** Nothing imitates a material. No leather, no brushed
metal, no paper texture. A skeuomorphic control says *I am a real object*;
these say *I am part of an instrument*, which is a weaker and more honest
claim.

**Not neumorphism.** Depth does not come from soft extrusion, and hierarchy
does not come from shadow. Two surfaces at slightly different lightness with a
1px frame between them read as two levels; the same two surfaces with a blurred
shadow read as one surface with a bruise.

**Not retro pastiche.** No CRT scanline overlay, no chromatic aberration, no
deliberately misaligned pixels. The reference is an instrument that was
*designed well in 1985*, not a photograph of a worn one.

## Is

**A moulded shell containing screens.** The page is the device: light plastic,
frames, notches. The dark surfaces inside it are its displays. Getting that
inversion right is most of the work — a dark page with light cards is a
different language entirely.

**Status you read before you read.** Diodes carry state at a glance. A label
explains what the diode already said.

**Density without noise.** Small type, wide tracking, tight rhythm. What makes
it legible is the discipline about movement: nothing pulses, nothing flashes,
highlights are capped at 1.4× resting luminance.

## The test

If you removed all the colour and kept only the frames, grooves and radii,
would it still read as a device? If yes, the structure is doing the work. If it
collapses into rectangles, the colour was carrying it and the design is
decoration.
